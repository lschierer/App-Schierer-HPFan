A people who are consumed by envy and lust. They have particularly strong
inheritances from the Thrones, reasonable inheritances from the Virtues and
Powers. Some have partial inheritances of random other types. Hags are known
for using infant sacrifice in evil magical rites. These sins robbed their
ancestors of all they can now value.
