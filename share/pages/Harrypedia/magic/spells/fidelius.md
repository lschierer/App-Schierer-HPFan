## Classification

charm

## Description

A secret is stored within the soul of the "Secret Keeper," who is the only person thenceforth who can divulge or share the secret.
If the "Secret Keeper" dies, all who have been given the secret become co-equal "Secret Keepers."  This weakens the charm in some
unspecified way.
