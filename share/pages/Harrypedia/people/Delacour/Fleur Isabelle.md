I do not like Fleur, but, thanks to fan fiction author [hp_fangal], I do understand her, at least a bit.[^260507-2]  

Fleur has faced incredible prejudice in her life, most of it because she has a [veela] grandmother, but part of it because she is, a beautiful woman.[^260507-1]  Unfortunately, the self-defence mechanism that she has developed in response to the unthinking unreasoning dislike that she faces from the other girls and women she meets is a projected self-confidence that comes across as arrogance.  How much of it is a *projected* front and how much of it truly is arrogance is an unanswerable question.  Mrs. Rowling has not given us that level of insight into Fleur's character.
