A breed of winged horse that possesses a conditional power of invisibility.[^260330-1]  That is, it is invisible to all except those who both possess magic, have personally seen death, and have internalized/understood it. The nature of this condition has caused many to consider the thestral unlucky.[^260330-2]  Thestrals are universally black.[^260330-3]
