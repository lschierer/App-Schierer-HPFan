These pairings are established in canon but we know relatively little about
them.

### Luna and Rolf Scamander

[Luna] married [Rolf Scamander]; both are naturalists.[^LSRS] This is one of
the few pairings that seems genuinely well-matched — two people with shared
passions and compatible temperaments.

### Percy and Audrey

[Percy] married the unknown [Audrey].[^PWA] [Percy] ends up head of the
Department of Transportation.[^PWJob] We know essentially nothing about
[Audrey] or their relationship.
